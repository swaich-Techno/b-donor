# B Donor Safety Notes

B Donor is a voluntary donor connector. It must not claim to sell blood, buy blood, store blood, collect blood, test blood, distribute blood, or perform transfusion.

Required disclaimer:

```text
B Donor only helps connect voluntary blood donors with patients/hospitals. Blood collection, testing, transfusion, and storage must be handled only by licensed hospitals/blood banks/medical professionals.
```

## Blood Donation Boundaries

- Use "request blood support" and "connect with voluntary donors".
- Do not use buy/sell wording.
- Do not charge per blood unit.
- Do not take commission per successful donation.
- Do not charge for donor phone reveal.
- Do not pay donors.
- Do not give cash, coupons, crypto, discounts, or cash-like benefits for donation.
- Do not allow paid ranking in emergency donor matching.
- Do not expose donor phone before donor accepts.
- Do not expose live location before donor accepts and starts tracking.

## AI Assistant Boundaries

- It cannot diagnose.
- It cannot prescribe.
- It cannot replace a doctor.
- It can ask follow-up questions.
- It can flag red flags and urgent symptoms.
- It can generate a doctor discussion summary.
- It can summarize reports in simple language.
- It must tell users that only approved doctors can create prescriptions through B Donor.
- Emergency symptoms should be routed to emergency hospital care first.

## Privacy Boundaries

- Donor phone is hidden from requester until donor accepts.
- Exact donor coordinates are not shown publicly.
- Live donor tracking starts only after request acceptance and donor consent.
- Live tracking expires after completion, cancellation, donor stop, or session expiry.
- Medical reports are private by default.
- Patients must consent before sharing AI summaries or medical reports with providers.
- Location, medical data, donor, WhatsApp/SMS, appointment sharing, certificate visibility, and tracking consent are stored explicitly.

## Donation Completion

- Donor confirms voluntary donation and no money, gift, crypto, coupon, or cash-like benefit.
- Patient confirms blood support was received and no money or payment was demanded.
- Hospital may witness when available.
- Admin can verify or reject.
- Disputes block certificate/reward completion until investigation.
- Verified completion starts 90-day donor cooldown.

## DONOR COIN Impact Points

- Phase 1 DONOR COIN means non-cash impact points only.
- It is not payment for blood donation.
- It is not cryptocurrency in Phase 1.
- It is not transferable, withdrawable, tradable, or redeemable.
- It has no monetary value.
- It has no investment or profit promise.

## Certificates

- Certificates verify B Donor platform records only.
- Public verification should mask names by default.
- Verified donor certificate does not prove medical eligibility on donation date.
- Voluntary donation certificate should not expose patient identity unless patient consents.
- Admin can revoke or mark certificates disputed.

## Monetization Rules

Allowed:

- SaaS subscriptions for doctors, hospitals, NGOs, corporates, and patients
- Manual billing in Phase 1
- CSR awareness/certificate sponsorship
- Aggregated anonymized analytics
- White-label software, training, support
- Future appointment platform convenience fee after confirmed booking flow exists

Forbidden:

- Selling blood
- Buying blood
- Blood-unit commission
- Successful donation commission
- Charging patient for donor contact
- Paid priority blood access
- Selling donor database or medical data
- Secret referral kickback from doctors/hospitals
- Ads inside emergency request or live tracking flow
